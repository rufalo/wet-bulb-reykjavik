What is wet bulb? ▾

The wet bulb temperature is the lowest temperature air can be cooled to by evaporating water into it — what a thermometer wrapped in a wet cloth would read. Dry air gives a big gap below the air temperature; at 100% humidity they are equal.

Data: latest hourly observation from the Icelandic Met Office (Veðurstofa Íslands) station in Reykjavík. If that is unavailable, Open-Meteo model data is used. Wet bulb is calculated from temperature, humidity and air pressure using the psychrometric equation.
